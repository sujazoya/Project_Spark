PROJECT SPARK — LEVEL 65 THE FACTORY THINKS — 3D ART KIT

This pack follows the modular common-origin arrangement of the supplied Level 59 art collection.
38 individual geometry FBX and 38 matching GLB models, four additional product variants, an assembled showcase in each format.

Unity:
1. Extract the ZIP. Copy Models/*.fbx (38 numbered equipment models) into your Project Spark Unity Assets art folder.
2. Copy Unity_Optional/SparkLevel65ArtPlacementEditor.cs into an Editor folder.
3. Select your existing Level 65 layout root.
4. Run Tools > Project Spark > Levels > 65 > Place HD Art Under Selected Layout.
5. The helper creates L65_HD_VISUAL_ART and places the 38 equipment models at their common origin.
6. Use Products_*.fbx as optional spawned dynamic products. Do not add the assembled showcase alongside individual models.
7. Consult Animation_Rigs.csv for named rig pivots. Asset FBX geometry is modular; wiring, live robot drives and PLC must be implemented separately.

IMPORTANT: Art only. No SparkTerminal, 66 wires, 104 sockets, robot physics, machine vision, PLC sequencing, or objectives included.
Models are procedurally generated with material-color regions, not artist-authored production PBR textures.
Unity FBX import and Play Mode were not tested in this environment.
