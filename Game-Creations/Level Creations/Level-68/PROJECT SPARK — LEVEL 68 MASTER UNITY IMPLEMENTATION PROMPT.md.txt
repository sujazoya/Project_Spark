# 9. FLUID MASS AND ENERGY CONSERVATION

For each liquid circuit, implement a consistent mass and energy balance.

## MASS

`MassNext = MassCurrent + (MassIn - MassOut)`

using integrated mass flows over the simulation interval.

## INTERNAL ENERGY

For a constant-specific-heat approximation:

`U = m * cp * T`

with a consistent temperature reference.

## FLOW ENERGY

Inflow and outflow must carry appropriate thermal enthalpy.

## IMPORTANT

Do not transfer heat between fluids by simply averaging their temperatures.

The exchanger must transfer an energy quantity, removing it from the hotter stream and adding it to the colder stream, subject to defined losses and capacity limits.

`QdotActual = Effectiveness * Cmin * (ThotIn - TcoldIn)`
