PROJECT SPARK — LEVEL 17 CREATION GUIDE
=======================================
LEVEL: Series–Parallel LED Network (5 V)

IMPORTANT: The uploaded Level_17 asset still had levelId=level_16 and
the old Level 16 series-LED targets. The accompanying import TXT corrects these.

CIRCUIT TO BUILD
  Battery +5V
      |
  Power Switch
      |
  R_LIMIT (replaceable: initially 220 Ω; player installs 100 Ω)
      |
      +---- R_RED 330 Ω ---- Red LED anode -> cathode ----+
      |                                                 |
      +---- R_GREEN 470 Ω -- Green LED anode -> cathode --+
                                                        |
                                                   Battery −

IMPORTANT: These are TWO PARALLEL LED BRANCHES after a COMMON series resistor.
Do not put the two LEDs in series. Do not connect the parallel resistors directly
to ground without their LEDs.

LEVEL PREFAB SETUP
1. Create or edit ORIGINAL Level_17_Layout prefab, not its Play Mode clone.
2. Keep the existing power supply, switch, LEDs, and their fixed binding IDs:
   led, led_2, PowerSupply_01.
3. Place two additional fixed resistors: 330 Ω (red branch) and 470 Ω
   (green branch). Bind them to R_RED_Component and R_GREEN_Component.
4. Bind their terminals:
   R_RED_Input / R_RED_Output
   R_GREEN_Input / R_GREEN_Output
5. Keep the runtime workstation slot Register_Slot_2 for the replaceable
   100 Ω series resistor. The initial wrong resistor is 220 Ω.
6. Configure SparkLevelWorkstationBindingBridge for Register_Slot_2
   and runtime component ID R_LIMIT_Component with terminal IDs
   R_LIMIT_Input / R_LIMIT_Output. Declare these IDs in the ORIGINAL
   prefab's SparkLevelSceneBindings runtime arrays.
7. Verify the original SparkLevelSceneBindings includes the four fixed
   branch resistor terminal IDs and the two fixed component IDs.
8. Import Level_17_SeriesParallel_Import.txt onto the ORIGINAL Level_17
   SparkLevelDefinition asset using your existing TXT importer.
9. Test after the 100 Ω replacement with switch ON and closed return.
10. Run source-current audit; calibrate target current ranges to measured
    SparkLED values before publishing.

NOMINAL ESTIMATES (assuming each LED ~2.0 V)
   Node after 100 Ω: ~4.03 V
   Red 330 Ω branch: ~6.16 mA
   Green 470 Ω branch: ~4.33 mA
   Source: ~10.49 mA
   Common resistor drop: ~1.05 V

The SparkLED nonlinear solver may differ. Do NOT rely on the earlier
concept image's 15 mA estimate; it is not consistent with this topology
and a ~2 V drop on each LED.

TARGETS (5)
- Red LED conducting: led
- Green LED conducting: led_2
- Red 330 Ω branch current: R_RED_Component, 4.5–7.5 mA
- Green 470 Ω branch current: R_GREEN_Component, 3.0–5.5 mA
- Total source current: PowerSupply_01, 8–12 mA

NOTES
- Target component IDs are binding aliases, not GameObject names.
- The TXT importer updates the level definition, not the scene prefab.
- Ensure branch resistor IDs and terminal IDs are declared/bound before
  testing; otherwise targets can remain false even with a working circuit.
