## STATE 1 — COMPRESSOR SUCTION

- Low-side pressure.
- Suction temperature.
- Suction enthalpy.
- Vapor phase.
- Superheat.
- Liquid carryover risk.

## STATE 2 — COMPRESSOR DISCHARGE

- High-side pressure.
- Discharge temperature.
- Discharge enthalpy.
- Compressor work.

## STATE 3 — CONDENSER OUTLET

- High-side pressure.
- Liquid-line temperature.
- Liquid enthalpy.
- Subcooling.

## STATE 4 — EXPANSION VALVE OUTLET

- Low-side pressure.
- Expansion outlet enthalpy.
- Two-phase quality.
- Mass flow to evaporator.

For the two-branch configuration, track branch-specific expansion and evaporator states where required.

Do not force both evaporators to have identical temperatures or mass flow when their loads and valve positions differ.
