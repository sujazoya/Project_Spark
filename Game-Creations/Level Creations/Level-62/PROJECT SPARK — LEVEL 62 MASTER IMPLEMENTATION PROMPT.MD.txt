## IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The DVR compensates voltage in series with the protected load.

It is not a shunt harmonic filter.

Do not reuse Level 61's active harmonic filter as a drop-in replacement for the DVR.

Reuse only compatible infrastructure such as phase measurement, converter switching and protection.

## CORE VOLTAGE EQUATION

For each phase:

`Vload_phasor = Vsource_phasor + Vinjected_phasor - Vseries_losses`

- `Vsource_phasor` is the disturbed source voltage.
- `Vinjected_phasor` is the series compensation.
- `Vseries_losses` represents transformer/filter and feeder drops.

A simplified ideal transformer model may omit losses initially, but limitations must be documented.

The output voltage must emerge from the network calculation.

Do not do:

`LoadVoltage = TargetVoltage`
