## Critical distinction

- Generator inertias.
- Generator dispatch.
- Initial rotor angles.
- Mechanical input.
- Generator internal voltage.
- Feeder impedance.
- Fault location.
- Fault impedance.
- Protection operation.
- Post-fault network.
- Damping.
- Stability criterion.

## Required implementation

1. Capture the initial stable operating state.
2. Choose a trial clearing time.
3. Simulate the fault-on interval.
4. Apply the required breaker operations.
5. Simulate the post-fault response.
6. Determine stable or unstable outcome.
7. Repeat with additional clearing times.
8. Narrow the stability boundary.

## Stability test
