# 15. RELATIVE POLISHING VELOCITY

This is a critical part of CMP physics.

The local sliding speed must be calculated from the relative motion between the rotating platen and rotating carrier.

## CONCEPTUAL EQUATION

For wafer point i:

`Vrelative_i = Magnitude(Vplaten_at_contact_i - Vcarrier_at_contact_i)`

where:

`Vplaten = OmegaPlaten × RadiusFromPlatenAxis`

`Vcarrier = OmegaCarrier × RadiusFromCarrierAxis`

Use consistent world-space vectors.

## IMPORTANT

Platen and carrier have different rotation centers.

Do not calculate relative polishing velocity from the absolute RPM difference alone.

Compute the contact-point kinematics correctly.

## REQUIRED BEHAVIOR

Changing either motor speed alters local sliding speed.

Different wafer positions can have different local velocities.

This affects regional material removal rates and uniformity.

# 23. PRESTON-LAW MATERIAL REMOVAL

Use a reduced-order educational CMP model.

## BASE EQUATION

`MRR_i = Kpreston_i * Pressure_i * RelativeVelocity_i`

where:

- MRR is local material removal rate.
- Kpreston is the calibrated material/process coefficient.
- Pressure is actual local contact pressure.
- RelativeVelocity is local sliding speed.

## EXTENDED GAME MODEL

`MRR_i = Kpreston_i * Pressure_i * RelativeVelocity_i * Fslurry_i * Fpad_i * Ftemperature_i`

Use bounded, documented factors.

## REQUIRED CONDITIONS

Material removal occurs only when:

- Wafer present.
- Wafer clamped.
- Wafer contacts pad.
- Valid safety permission exists.
- Platen rotates.
- Carrier operation is valid.
- Slurry conditions are acceptable.
- Pad has usable condition.
- Process is in POLISHING state.

## IMPORTANT

Do not let a fixed timer remove material while the platen is stopped or the wafer is raised.
