### 5 — Educational energy model and formal test scenario
Use supplied EnergyTeachingDemo only as optional non-scoring idealized accounting. Example game configuration: 240 Wh, initial SOC 80%, solar 180 W, essential 60 W, auxiliary 45 W, charge efficiency 0.92, discharge efficiency 0.90, reserve 25%. Sunlight 55 min at both load powers gives 63.25 Wh net stored increase before clamp. These are idealized game values, not hardware ratings. Authoritative O4 must use integration from the mapped electrical system, not demo outputs. Validate conservation including losses, capacity clamps, spilled surplus and unmet demand. - **F2 R1: Battery B current-sense drift.** Measured charge disagrees with independent energy ledger. Approved shunt repair and independent signed-current calibration. Require correct mapped part, power-safe intervention, restored topology, fresh test and independent safety check.
- **F3 C1: Essential bus filter ESR rise.** Excess bus ripple under load step. Approved capacitor replacement and ripple/load-step test. Require correct mapped part, power-safe intervention, restored topology, fresh test and independent safety check.
