# Level 84 AAA-inspired art delivery notes

Procedurally generated original 3D geometry for an attitude-control arena featuring three gimbal rings, detailed spacecraft/solar wings, four flywheel assemblies, three magnetorquer coil systems, star-tracker cameras, gyroscopes, instrument racks and a celestial backdrop. All files are true mesh exports; they are NOT screenshots or 2D placeholders.

Art remains a polished procedural blockout/tech-art kit, not final artist-sculpted production AAA art. UV layout, baked normal/roughness/occlusion channels, collision meshes, LODs, GPU instancing, C# compilation and Play Mode verification remain future production work.
