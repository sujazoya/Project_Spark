# Evidence and draft generation

The v19 final coverage CSV is the baseline for the 130 levels. Use **Project Spark → SUPER AI → Universal Auto Draw → Audit v20 Construction Evidence** to export the current read-only evidence and populate the runtime preview resource.

The audit checks level and layout identity, the binding table, unique approved plan selection, native pair structure, and exact endpoint declarations. It records endpoints that need an installed runtime component and computes the authored revision. This does not establish circuit validity, physical reach, correct placement, or mission completion for draft plans.

The Unity 6.3 audit resolved 130 unique levels. The ordered pairs for all 62 v19 drafts matched current sources and passed structural checks. Level 20 has eight slot-dependent endpoints. The audit recognized the two reviewed starter plans and left all 62 drafts unapproved.

Levels 18 and 19 name their complete divider topology in their level definitions. The importer maps that topology to the bound switch, source and resistor terminals. The separate action drafts add source isolation, authored workstation IDs and resistor values, native wire order, electrical checks, and official evaluator requirements. Neither action draft authorizes automated placement or power-on.
