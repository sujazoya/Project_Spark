# Remaining blockers

- Draft plans require full authored procedures, explicit approval, and native Play Mode evidence.
- Levels without terminal-pair recipes need evidence from their level definition, mission controller, board process, or other authoritative level records. A binding name alone cannot determine a connection.
- Board production and diagnostic actions require typed authorization and native adapters. An electrical wire pair list cannot represent board assembly.
- Later engineering records and Level 120 certification require case-specific review before native execution.
