## 04. Functional behavior and source-of-truth data

1. **Component assembly**: expose stable `component_ref`, `kind`, `part_spec_id`, `footprint_id`, `board_id`, `orientation`, `pin_one`, pad list, solder status, component health, state revision and source manifest hash. Reject wrong component, swapped phase drivers, missing lead and insufficient solder. Never auto-install from assembled art.
2. **Electrical graph**: connect approved physical pads using existing `SparkTerminal` and `SparkCircuitSystem`; motor U/V/W phase nodes and negative DC return must be separate. Do not infer electrical nets from PBR copper art. Preserve branch shorts/opens and power capacity constraints.
3. **DC-link commissioning**: model DC-link capacitance and precharge, contactor/switch power enable, UV/OV protection, ground/return continuity, discharge policy and energy in joules. Values and voltage limits require approved source design; avoid suggesting player use real high-voltage hardware. The virtual level uses an educational bounded simulation only.
4. **Gate drive and inverter**: model three half bridges, per-phase high-/low-side switches, dead-time constraints, isolation and fault gating, phase current sensing, DC-bus load and switching losses at a bounded rate (not necessarily switching microsteps). Use approved device ratings and measurable states; never command torque from a mere UI toggle.
5. **Motor and load**: have an independently modeled virtual PMSM/BLDC motor (educational equivalent), rotor inertia, damping, back EMF, load torque, speed and position limits. Determine current/torque/position through explicit correct control transforms and unit-consistent machine parameters.
6. **FOC**: deterministic Clarke/Park transform convention, current d/q loops with gains/saturation, speed loop with anti-windup, inverse transform, PWM duty limiting and a configurable sample schedule. Tie control success to independent measured source evidence, not perfect illustrative sinusoids.
7. **Encoder + resolver**: maintain independent primary and plausibility feedback. Fault F2 changes the approved true contact graph; stale or ambiguous sensor feedback inhibits torque or triggers controlled derate per policy.
8. **STO A/B**: two independently qualified safety channels must inhibit gate commands with defined fault latency and reset rules. Treat any failure as `TORQUE_DISABLED`. Model as educational safety logic, *not* IEC 61800-5-2 or machinery safety certification.
9. **Regenerative braking**: torque direction and mechanical energy transfer affect DC bus, chopper duty, brake resistor losses/temperature and hardware overvoltage; a false brake sensor may not be trusted as proof of energy dissipation.
10. **Telemetries**: timestamp and label DC-link voltage, phase U/V/W currents, rotor speed/angle, q-axis current, PWM enable, STO A/B state, brake temperature, active fault IDs and evidence generation IDs.

## 06. Three independent recoverable faults

**F1 — Phase V high-side gate path open:** approved U12/Qx target (pin **UNASSIGNED**). Opening a gate-drive contact distorts phase currents and q-axis torque; use virtual multimeter/oscilloscope/net tracer; repair correct real connection and rerun phase balance, FOC and protection evidence. Do not infer one specific transistor ref until a schematic assigns it.

**F2 — Encoder differential via intermittency:** exact via, net, layer and signal pin **UNASSIGNED**. A deterministic intermittent contact degrades rotor position and speed plausibility; use encoder waveform and layer viewer, repair approved via, and demonstrate *fresh* position and torque qualification. Merely hiding diagnostics cannot mark this fixed.

**F3 — Regenerative brake feedback thermal drift:** U14 / sensor candidate physical pin **UNASSIGNED**. Brake telemetry drifts during regeneration; impose real overvoltage/thermal protection where appropriate, find and repair the approved sensor path/component, then rerun braking under safe bounded conditions and validate new measurements.

All faults must be staged after base qualification and replayable with a recorded seed. Each repair must be a transaction validated by selected virtual tool, actual Collider target, circuit authority, supply preconditions and state mutation. Visual VFX may follow success but must not decide success.
