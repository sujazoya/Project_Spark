PROJECT SPARK LEVEL 129
The Silicon Workshop / H610-EDU-1
Validation completed 22 November 2026 in Unity 6.3 LTS (6000.3.19f1).

Campaign and visual assets
The normal MainMenu campaign loaded Level_129_Layout and the manager evaluated all six objectives from an empty, isolated service bench. The catalog links the original prefab GUID to the new Level 129 definition. The original 121 campaign entries remained in order, with only the new entry appended to the saved scene.
The board is centered on the workshop table. Its measured presentation footprint is 9.42 × 7.98 Unity units on the 15.79 × 8.54 table. The 200 × 236.16 mm substrate, original service anchors and 451 factory, structural and removable placements retain the source coordinates. The kit audit validated 456 modular FBX/native mesh pairs, two complete models, 19 source PBR families and 57 texture files.
H610-EDU-1 is a fictional bench circuit. Its 22 placement and test-point records are in H610ME/Data/H610_EDU_1_Test_Points.csv. Physical motherboard traces, pinouts and manufacturer firmware remain outside the simulation. The training drive is a required part of this mission so players can repeat the storage fault and integrity tests.

Play Mode acceptance
1 PASS Native pointer selection reached the original CPU footprint. CPU seating and final retention are separate. Tested keyed orientation, DDR4 rejection, both DDR5 latches, the cooler's cross-clamp and the M.2 retaining screw. The independent service fixture awarded assembly with every motherboard output off. Restart restored seated hardware and required fresh qualification.
2 PASS Loaded 5 V standby was required before main power. Measured EPS at 12.000 V / 80.0 mA, CPU at 1.100 V / 110.0 mA, DDR5 at 1.100 V / 33.3 mA and I/O at 3.300 V / 27.5 mA through the native solver.
3 PASS Fresh CPU, dual-channel memory, video and storage POST completed. Interface tests stayed blocked before a passing POST.
4 PASS 12/12 samples each on PCIe ×16, PCIe ×1, M.2, SATA, HDMI, VGA, USB and LAN. Rejected the wrong fixture protocol on a powered interface.
5 PASS PQ04 stopped POST at CPU with 12.00 V EPS and 0.00 V VCORE. DDR5_2 stopped at DRAM with one trained channel and one failed. M2_1 stopped at BOOT with 6/12 readable blocks. Verified isolation, rejected a wrong footprint and spare, and required fresh solver readings, POST and eight interface passes after each repair.
6 PASS Native loaded VCORE and fan feedback were present before the fan opened. The latch dropped CPU load with standby retained. A powered repair and premature reset were blocked. The explicit reset required electrical and POST requalification. All 24 final thermal samples passed at 1200 RPM, with a 37.7 °C peak and 34.1 °C after recovery. Both trace lines were inspected in the final 6/6 screenshot.

The automated Unity Editor run used the campaign loader, native selection ray, electrical solver and objective manager. Standalone builds, physical touch input, performance profiling and full earlier-level gameplay regression were outside this run. The complete Play Mode report and screenshots are in Logs/Level129*.
