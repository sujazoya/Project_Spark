2 PASS The project's measurement system emitted real probe readings at standby and on both sides of EPS. Before reseating, standby was 5.00 V, upstream EPS was 12.00 V, downstream EPS was 0.00 V and VCORE was 0.00 V. The matched isolated reseat recovered approximately 12.00 V at the socket and 1.10 V at VCORE. Six fresh loaded readings qualified.
3 PASS Powered virtual ROM stopped POST before memory training. Two faulty reads returned F09E681A and 58035FF9. The isolated training-image recovery produced two repeatable 7408CC4E reads and a clean cold POST. Repair without fresh POST was rejected.
4 PASS A separate isolated restart was required after ROM recovery. Both DDR5 channels passed 64/64 pattern reads each, PCIe ×16 and ×1 passed 12/12 training patterns each, and NVMe passed 12/12 block CRC checks.
5 PASS POST succeeded with 1200 RPM motor motion and 0 RPM readback. Three consecutive missing-tach samples under solved CPU load triggered a cutoff. CPU and soak current dropped while standby stayed live. The original site required isolated inspection and the matching tach spare plus an explicit reset. Fresh device and thermal qualification passed after the repair.
6 PASS The final audit rejected reuse of the previous repair boot and demanded fresh power, POST, five devices and thermal evidence from the same later cold start. All 28 samples passed across two load pulses and cooldown. Peak 38.0 °C, recovery 34.0 °C, tach 1200 RPM. The three case journals retained ordered before/after evidence tied to the repair revision, topology and boot.
